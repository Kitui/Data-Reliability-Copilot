# Contract Validation Findings Registry

Validation findings are displayed directly beneath the Contract Registry for the selected contract.

- Shows finding, column, constraint, expected value, observed result, affected rows, dataset version, and audit context.
- Displays up to six findings per page.
- Previous and Next controls navigate additional findings.
- Selecting another contract resets findings to page one.
- The contract detail panel remains focused on metadata, actions, and the contract definition.
- No database migration is required.
